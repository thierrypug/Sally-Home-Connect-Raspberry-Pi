# Sally Home Connect — Raspberry Pi

🇫🇷 [Version française](README.md)

**Your smart home, no rewiring, and your data stays at home.**

Sally Home Connect controls your lights, plugs, shutters and heating from your phone and computer,
**100% locally**: no online account, no cloud, no data from your home ever leaves it.

This version installs on a **Raspberry Pi**, which stays on day and night, with no screen or keyboard.
Would you rather try it on your computer first? See the [Windows beta](https://github.com/thierrypug/Sally-Home-Connect-Windows-Beta).

## What Sally can do

- A simple dashboard, on phone, tablet and computer
- Lights, plugs, roller shutters, heating (pilot wire and thermostats), sensors
- **Guided device setup**: choose what you are adding, then the brand, and Sally reads the right pairing steps aloud
- Routines ("when… if… then…"), scenes, weekly heating schedule
- Home modes: home, away, night, holiday (with simulated presence)
- Energy use and its cost (peak and off-peak hours)
- Alerts spoken aloud: water leak, smoke, low battery, device not responding
- **Access code** and family accounts, time-limited guest access, invitation by QR code
- **Voice control in 5 languages that works without the Internet**: your voice never leaves the house
- **Family life**: messages between family members, reminders, silent wake-up, "good night", "I'm leaving / I'm home", shared shopping list
- **Sally saves energy for you**: heating cut when a window stays open, forgotten lights switched off, monthly savings in euros
- **Control from outside** through your router's VPN, with no outside service (fixed address set automatically)
- One-file backup, one-tap updates, "Shut down" button

## Compatible hardware

| Technology | Supported USB sticks |
|---|---|
| Zigbee | Sonoff ZBDongle-P, Sonoff ZBDongle-E, ConBee II |
| EnOcean | EnOcean USB 300 |

Several thousand Zigbee devices are recognised (Philips Hue, IKEA, Aqara, Sonoff, NodOn, Legrand…), thanks to a
library shipped with Sally: no online lookup. EnOcean modules tested: NodOn (battery-free switches, window contacts,
shutters).

## Truly local home automation

- No online account, no subscription, no cloud service.
- Everything works without the Internet.
- Sally never goes online without your consent. Only the weather and update checks can, if you allow it in Settings.
